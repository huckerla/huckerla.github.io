Last week, I had the pleasure of participating in the autumn school of the collaborative research centre [SFB 1294](https://www.sfb1294.de/events/event/autumn-school-2026), which has been funding me since the beginning of this month. It took place in a beautiful location on the Baltic Sea, where I had the great opportunity to meet and get to know my future colleagues.

Afterwards, I attended the annual workshop of the DFG research unit [FOR 5381](https://for5381.uni-freiburg.de/en/events/workshop-at-01-02-10-2026-in-bochum/) in Bochum, where I was a proud member of [Project 2](https://for5381.uni-freiburg.de/projekte/optimal-actions-and-stopping-in-sequential-learning-de/) until the end of September. During my PhD, the research unit provided me with many interesting talks and a lot of scientific input, but even more importantly, with many inspiring discussions and wonderful colleagues, for which I am very grateful. I hope to stay connected with all those wonderful people in the future!
